## Thursday 29th October afternoon: trip to the Mont St-Michel

On Thursday afternoon, a trip will be organized to the **Mont St-Michel**, giving
participants time together outside the scientific sessions.

There will be a bus that brings us from the Inria convention center to the Mont
Saint-Michel. We will walk a bit, so bring good shoes and a wind coat.
